# XWALK

**Pixelated European-style crosswalk game** featuring the iconic **Red Man** and **Green Man** (Ampelmännchen).

**Side-scroller** (left → right) • primarily a **touch-screen phone game**.

## Play

**https://htmlpreview.github.io/?https://github.com/jmm5101/XWALK/blob/main/index.html**

### How to play

1. Choose **GREEN MAN** or **RED MAN**.
2. **Green Man** — hop left-to-right across the single horizontal piano-key crosswalk using the big on-screen buttons (or tap the screen). Reach the right sidewalk in 60 seconds. Miss the keys (blacktop) or get hit by a car = run over.
3. **Red Man** — classic akimbo (arms out) stop pose. He does **not** move. He stands still and times out after 60 seconds.

### Design
- True left-to-right side-scroller layout.
- Single continuous piano-key crosswalk stripe.
- Cars stay on the asphalt and never drive over the painted keys.
- Larger pixel sprites.
- Touch-first controls (big buttons + tap).
- SAM Coupé inspired chunky look.
